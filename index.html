Sachi Edirisinghe

Human-Robot Interaction Researcher | Real-World Robot Deployment Studies

I am a Human–Robot Interaction (HRI) researcher working on the development and real-world evaluation of interactive service robot systems. My research combines robotic system development, field studies, and behavioral analysis to investigate human–robot interaction in real-world social settings. I received my PhD in informatics from Kyoto University Japan in 2024.

During my PhD, I investigated how service robots can intervene appropriately in public spaces in response to social norm violations. In my postdoctoral research, I examined how Vision-Language Models (VLMs) can enable more natural robot behavior in public spaces and influence people's interactions with robots.

I am seeking postdoctoral and faculty positions in academia, as well as research roles in industry, with a focus on robotics and human-robot interaction. I am also open to research collaborations in these areas.
